How your data is handled

Your progress

Lingorun saves your progress — XP, coins, characters, quests, statistics, settings and the words you got wrong — on this phone, so the game works fully offline.

Your account

If your device is signed into Game Center, Lingorun creates an account for you in the background using the identifier Apple gives us — no name, no e-mail, no form. Your progress is then also stored on our servers so it follows you to your other Apple devices. Turn Game Center off in iOS Settings and everything stays on this phone.

How the game is played

We record what happens in the game — races started and finished, levels unlocked, quests completed, daily goals met — to see which lessons work. Each of those records carries a random id for your device and for the app session, plus the app version and platform, so we can tell one player's run from another's without knowing who you are. It goes to our own servers and nowhere else.

No tracking, no ads

There are no ads, no advertising identifiers and no third-party trackers or analytics services. Nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

Purchases

Coin packs and the Lingorun Plus subscription are sold by the Apple App Store or Google Play; the store handles payment, and we never see your card or billing address. So that a purchase can be credited and restored on a new phone, purchases need an account, and RevenueCat — the service that checks store receipts for us — tells our servers what was bought: the product, price, currency, store, time and the store's transaction ids, linked to your account. That purchase history is kept as long as your account exists (it is what a refund or a restore is checked against).

Permissions

The only permission Lingorun asks for is notifications, and only if you switch on the daily reminder in Settings. Haptic feedback uses the standard system vibration and can be switched off too.

Reminders

Daily reminders are planned on your phone from when you usually race; nothing about that leaves the device. If you have an account and reminders are on, Lingorun also stores the notification address of this phone (the push token) with your interface language, time zone and usual hour, so that after two weeks and after a month away it can send one "come back" message each — never more, and never while you are playing. Switching reminders off, signing out or deleting the account removes that address from our servers.

Deleting your data

“Reset progress” in Settings clears every saved value on this phone and in your account. “Delete account” under Settings → Account removes the account and everything stored with it, including the device and session ids on the play records made from this phone — what stays behind is counted, not identifiable. Uninstalling the app removes what is on the phone.
